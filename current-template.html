Genesis Diya & Genesis Mira: Two Ski-In Ski-Out Developments, Le Lavachet, Tignes
Hi [firstname, fallback=there],

Two new developments by the same developer share one address: Le Lavachet, Tignes, with ski-in ski-out access to the Espace Killy ski area.

Both are turnkey, fully-managed five-star residences. We are sending them together because, for us, the location does the heavy lifting for both, and the real choice comes down to budget and timing.

What we like here is straightforward: the position, the access, and a managed five-star model that asks nothing of the owner. On those terms, we think both are worth a look.
Le Lavachet is one of Tignes' three high areas, alongside Tignes le Lac and Val Claret.

It sits inside Espace Killy, the Tignes-Val d'Isère domain, with around 300km of pistes. The Grande Motte glacier rises to 3,656m, the highest point of the ski area, reached by a funicular through the mountain for high-altitude, dual-season skiing.

Both developments are ski-in ski-out, and in our view this is the part that matters most: you are on the snow from the door. Tignes also offers a stronger price per square metre than neighbouring Val d'Isère, which is no small thing at this level.
 
Genesis Diya is the smaller, earlier-delivery option, and the one furthest along.

It comprises 27 apartments of 2 to 4 bedrooms, ranging from 69.55 to 171.85 sq.m, with delivery in June 2027. Prices start at EUR 1,205,000 excluding VAT, with five 4-bed apartments from EUR 2,100,000 to EUR 2,395,000.

Only 6 of the 27 apartments remain, with 21 already sold.

Owners have a cellar and ski lockers, plus a swimming pool, sauna and steam room. The residence is run by the developer as a fully managed, turnkey home, which is the appeal: you own it, but you are not running it.
 
Genesis Mira is the larger development, with a lower entry price and a longer runway.

It comprises 71 apartments of 1 to 5 bedrooms, ranging from 46 to 173 sq.m, with delivery in December 2028. Prices run from EUR 890,000 to EUR 3,615,000 excluding VAT, with approximately half the apartments still available.

Alongside the swimming pool, sauna and steam room, Mira adds a spa for treatments and massage, with five-star hotel services throughout. For a buyer in less of a hurry, we rate the extra time to plan as much as the lower entry point.

Both developments are off-plan, with delivery in 2027 and 2028, so they suit buyers planning ahead rather than those wanting immediate use.

Both residences are run by the developer as turnkey, fully-managed five-star homes, with shared wellness throughout: swimming pool, sauna and steam room.

With the Grande Motte glacier at 3,656m, the ski area runs across two seasons, so use is not limited to the winter months. That dual-season access is, for us, what sets the location apart.
Genesis Diya suits a buyer who wants the nearer delivery and a near-complete development. Genesis Mira opens at a lower price with more time to plan.

For us, both come down to the same things: where they sit, how easily you ski from them, and the fact that someone else runs them. On those grounds we think they are worth a look.
