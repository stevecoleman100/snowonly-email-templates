A new development has come to market in Samoëns that is worth a closer look. Chalets Bérénice offers something uncommon in the French Alps: freehold ownership with no rental obligation, a year-round heated swimming pool, and an entry price of EUR 405,000 for a new-build apartment in the Grand Massif.

The project is being delivered by MGM Constructeur, a developer with over 60 years in mountain real estate.

Chalets Bérénice comprises 50 apartments across five chalets, set on a south-facing plot with uninterrupted mountain views. Phase 1 (Chalets A, B, C) delivers 30 apartments, with Phase 2 (Chalets D, E) adding a further 20.

Every apartment comes with a fully fitted kitchen and bathrooms, covered parking, a private cellar, and a ski locker. Five penthouses are available, one per chalet, at 121 sqm with a 31.1 sqm balcony.

The development includes an indoor heated swimming pool, open all year. This is unusual for a freehold development with no management mandate. For buyers considering future resale, a communal pool adds measurable value to a property that carries no rental obligation.

Buyers also have the option to rent their apartment and reclaim 20% VAT, but this is entirely voluntary. There is no management company dictating usage.
