Watch Mark’s tour of Falcon (6 minutes)
Falcon is a hameau of stone-and-timber chalets set on the mountainside: 32 apartments and penthouses plus 3 chalets.
 
It sits about 150 metres from the Morel chairlift and 500 metres from the resort centre. The apartments face the Méribel valley, south-facing, for afternoon sun on the terraces.
 
Shared facilities include a swimming pool, a spa and wellness area, reception with concierge, a private shuttle, a ski shop and secure underground parking.
 
Because the residences are furnished and developer-managed, they can be let through the managed programme from the first season, or kept for your own use.
