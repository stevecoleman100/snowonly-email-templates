A new development has come to market in Samoëns that combines three things rarely found together: freehold ownership with no obligation to rent, a year-round heated swimming pool, and a starting price of EUR 405,000. Chalets Bérénice is a 50-apartment project by MGM, one of the most established developers in the French Alps with over 60 years in mountain construction.
The development comprises five south-facing chalets with uninterrupted mountain views. Every apartment includes a fully fitted kitchen, covered parking, a private cellar, and a ski locker.

The ownership structure is straightforward: full freehold, no management mandate, no rental obligation. You use the property when you want. If you choose to rent, a 20% VAT reclaim option is available, but it is entirely optional.

What sets this project apart from most new builds in the northern Alps is the on-site indoor heated swimming pool, open all year. Developments without a rental mandate rarely include communal facilities at this level, which also strengthens long-term resale value.

Location
Samoëns is a traditional Savoyard village in the Grand Massif, one of the largest linked ski areas in France with 265 km of pistes connecting Samoëns, Les Carroz, and Flaine. Geneva Airport is approximately 1 hour 15 minutes by road.

The village sits at 800m, with lift access to 1,600m and the ski area reaching 2,500m at its summit. This suits buyers who value a year-round Alpine village with genuine summer appeal over a purpose-built ski station. Samoëns is regularly cited as one of the most attractive villages in the French Alps.
