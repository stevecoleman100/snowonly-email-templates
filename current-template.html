The Location
 
Courchevel Moriond (1650) sits in Les 3 Vallées, the world's largest ski area: 600km of pistes, 1,300m to 3,230m, seven connected resorts.
 
The 1650 village has strong sun exposure and runs quieter and more independent than 1850, with its own pharmacy, shops and restaurants.
 
Courchevel as a whole carries the Famille Plus label and holds eight Michelin-starred restaurants. The Aquamotion centre, between 1650 and 1850, adds pools, a spa, a climbing wall and a surf wave.
 
Neither development is true ski-in, ski-out. Both sit metres from the snow rather than directly on it, with the Maison de Moriond chairlift serving 1650. The position suits buyers who want walk-to-the-lift convenience in a full-facility village.
 
Steamboat
 
Steamboat is 25 lodges, 2 to 7 bedrooms, 66 to 266 m2, in the village centre.
 
It is 30m from the slopes: cross the road, up the escalator, onto the snow. It is the closest of the two to the chairlift.
 
Every lodge is turnkey with 5* hotel services: a swimming pool, sauna, steam room and spa for treatment and massage, 24/7 reception and concierge, room service, and a private ski room and ski-butler service.
 
The development has its own Savoyard restaurant and bar.
Sundance
 
Sundance is 44 lodges, 2 to 6 bedrooms, 67 to 328 m2, in the village centre.
 
It is 150m from the slopes, often less. Inside the development are shops and restaurants, including La Bokka, an Italian restaurant and terrace bar, plus underground parking and EV charging stations.
 
Every lodge is turnkey with the same 5* facilities: swimming pool, sauna, steam room and spa, 24/7 concierge, room service, and ski-butler service.
 
The top 6-bedroom lodge additionally includes a private cinema and spa.
